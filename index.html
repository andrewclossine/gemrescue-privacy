ENGLISH PRIVACY POLICY — GEM RESCUE Last updated: October 6, 2026 Gem Rescue ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by Andovexa ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and how. 1. WHAT WE COLLECT We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or exact location. Your game progress (levels, stars, gems, lives, settings, language) is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is removed when you uninstall the game. 2. ADVERTISING (GOOGLE ADMOB) The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads). More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy 3. SHARING We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with Google (AdMob) as described above. 4. CHILDREN The game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 5. SECURITY AND RETENTION Data we control stays on your device. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's own policies. 6. YOUR RIGHTS Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal data about you, please manage ad-related data through Google's tools and your device settings. 7. CHANGES We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. 8.
